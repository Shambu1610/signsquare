Our FAQs

The most common questions we get asked.

How long does a typical signage project take?

Most indoor signs and print jobs are ready within a few working days of design approval. Larger fabricated or illuminated outdoor signs usually take one to three weeks, depending on size, materials and permits.

Do you handle design, or do I need my own artwork?

Either works. Our in-house designers can create your identity from scratch, adapt your existing brand guidelines, or prepare your artwork for production. You'll get a mock-up to approve before we produce anything.

Do you install the signage as well?

Yes. Our team handles site surveys, fabrication and installation, including high-level façade work with lifting equipment.

Can you help with municipality approvals for outdoor signs?

We can prepare the drawings and documents needed for signage permits and advise you on the process for your location.

Can you brand an entire vehicle fleet?

Yes. We design and install full wraps, partial wraps and decals on vans, cars and trucks, and keep the branding consistent across the whole fleet.

How do I get a quote?

Send us your requirements, approximate sizes and a few reference photos using the form below or on WhatsApp. We usually reply with a quote within one working day.
